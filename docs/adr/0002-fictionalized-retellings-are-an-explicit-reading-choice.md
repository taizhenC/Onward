# Fictionalized retellings are an explicit reading choice

The owner permits invented scenes and events under real historical names and
selected the prominent label “Fictionalized retelling.” These are public authored
fiction, with a separate documented-background section; sources support only
that background, never invented dialogue or emotional turning points.

Release them as an explicitly chosen collection, not a silent fallback from an
emotional disclosure or as historical StorySpecs. The catalog and reader always
show the label, and readers acknowledge it before reading. Topic browsing is a
reading preference, not a confidence-scored emotional match. The collection
takes no disclosure, creates no account or saved Owner Story, and keeps reading
state only in the page. Its public text is not access-controlled by the notice.

This lets creative retellings be published without falsifying historical sources,
rewriting old gold labels, or disabling historical release checks. The held
historical stages and existing owner/session history remain unchanged. The
historical benchmark failures are not resolved or counted as fiction successes.
The existing incident switch pauses this collection too; crisis resources remain
available. Adding automatic fiction matching or account saving is separate work.
