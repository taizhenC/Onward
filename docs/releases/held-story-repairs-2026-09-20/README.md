## What changed

The owner asked to fix the gaps, permitted small modifications where evidence could not be recovered, and explicitly chose: **“Yes—use shorter vignettes where needed.”** That permits a format change, not invented history or approval of unread revisions.

These are short, source-led vignettes (157–327 words), not padded 700–950-word stories. Some follow a remembered experience; others follow a document or openly summarized sequence. Seven existing passage slots remain for compatibility. Their labels do not prove seven separate scenes, a deepest low, a failed attempt or a transforming moment.

The repair approach is concrete: reopen primary material where possible; identify when it is mediated by an institution or scholarly excerpt; remove disputed chronology and inaccessible-source dependencies; narrow claims to what was actually read. Missing history stays missing. No fictional thoughts, dialogue, cure or convenient resolution fills the gap. The per-story research notes distinguish inspected evidence from material not inspected.
