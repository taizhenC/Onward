# Held-story repairs — new review packet

Work in progress, 20 September 2026. This packet addresses the 26 remaining
historical-story holds after the Shelley and Brontë publication. Nothing here
is approved or published. The 24 live historical stories and all older review
snapshots remain unchanged.

## The owner's new direction

The owner asked to fix the gaps and permitted small modifications when missing
material cannot be found. In a follow-up, the owner explicitly chose:
**“Yes—use shorter vignettes where needed.”** This authorizes a limited format
change, not invented history and not approval of text that has not yet been read.

Where a source supports an experience but not a complete sequence of situated
scenes, a new draft may use a short, source-led vignette. It can retain an honest
summary, an explicit time jump, a remembered pattern, or an unresolved ending.
It must not manufacture a deepest low, a failed attempt, a private realization,
a cure, or an event that conveniently supplies the usual turning point.

The seven existing passage slots remain for reader compatibility. Their labels
are not evidence that seven separate events happened. Any departure from the
usual moment/length targets is stated in each story's research decision and
reading notes. The global recipe, runtime validators and matching rules are not
being weakened or edited. A mismatch with the installed episode or matching
data remains a separate integration decision, even when a vignette is readable.

## Scope and checks

- Set 1: Berlin, Butler, Carver, Ray Charles, Christie, Coleman, Coltrane,
  Jones and Lamarr (nine).
- Set 2: Frances Glessner Lee, Edmonia Lewis, Lindgren, McClintock, Owens,
  Poitier, Rachmaninoff, Rogers and Rudolph (nine).
- Set 3: Rustin, Sanders, Simone, Sullivan, Tallchief, Wang, Wilson and Yeats
  (eight).

Each repair must distinguish the former gap, the source actually inspected,
the claim removed or changed, and any issue still unresolved. Primary texts
are preferred; a catalog listing is not a read letter, and a search excerpt is
not a verified transcript. New statements need bounded source locators.
Retrospective accounts keep their memory/uncertainty qualifications.

Research findings are written beside the candidates. Exact-byte hashes,
reading copies and independent source/craft checks will identify the final
review targets. All candidates retain `status: draft` and `review: {}`.
Mechanical parsing, publication simulation, canonical composition and replay
are checks of structure, not proof of historical accuracy or human approval.

No production, database, credentials, provider configuration, installed
figure-library, recipe or matching changes are part of this repair request.
Publication would require review of these exact new texts and the established
separate release procedure.
