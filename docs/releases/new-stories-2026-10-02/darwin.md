## Scope and limitations

Darwin was twenty-two throughout late August and early September 1831. His August 30 letter says his father did not decidedly refuse but strongly advised against going; this draft preserves that distinction and does not call it a prohibition. The autobiography remembers refusing that evening, while the nearer diary and August 30 letter place the refusal the next morning. The draft follows the contemporary letter and nearer diary, avoiding an exact evening refusal. ## Reading copy

### 1. scene

At home, he received a letter offering a place on a voyage. He was twenty-two. The letter reached him late in the evening. A captain needed a companion who could collect and observe the natural world. He was not being presented as someone whose training was complete.

He wanted to accept. Collecting, watching, and writing things down were part of the offered work. He had an invitation in front of him. The words were there on the page. The offer could be read again.

### 2. dark_moment

His father gave strong advice against going. It was not a firm refusal. He still could not feel comfortable taking the offer against that advice. His father worried about whether he could settle into steady work afterward. There was his lack of experience at sea. There was the short time left to prepare.

There was the chance he might not suit the captain. His body and mind both needed preparation for such an undertaking. He wanted to go, but his father’s disapproval could take away the energy he needed. Without those objections, he was willing to take the risks. The offered place remained in the letter.

### 3. response

He wrote to refuse the offer. He explained the objections to the person who had recommended him. He thanked him for the trouble he had taken. Another letter carried his refusal to the person arranging the place. Even the address was hard to make out.

Help from the post office had not made it certain. He asked his correspondent to send a line with the same news. That could cover the chance of the letter not arriving. His answer was now written down. It was an answer against the offer he wanted.

### 4. struggle

He went to his uncle’s house at the end of the month. The household supported his going. That evening he wrote a list of his father’s objections. The list went beyond being ready for the captain. His father doubted whether the work had any use. He worried about another change of profession and uncomfortable living conditions.

His uncle wrote answers to the objections. He argued that the work had definite tasks and followed the young man’s interests. The young man sent the list and answers with another letter to his father. He asked for a clear yes or no. If the answer was no, he promised to give way and never raise it again. He did not want to go while his father remained unhappy about it.

### 5. turning_point

His uncle brought him to see his father. The offer had drawn a refusal and another request. Now the two men had arrived together. His father gave his consent kindly. He had changed his mind.

The uncle had answered the objections, but had left the choice to father and son. The father’s answer was now yes. The young man’s request for another hearing had received an answer. The refusal was still part of what had happened. The new answer had come in its place.

### 6. became

After reaching his lodging, he sent a note to the person who had recommended him. He hoped the place had not been given away. He was tired and going to bed. He asked when he could come the next morning. They spent a day considering what needed to be done. There was much to do.

Introductions could help him find people with useful experience. His adviser warned him against treating the matter as settled. He still needed long conversations with the captains. He asked his sister to keep the news quiet in case he did not go. Another letter carried that request home.

### 7. bridge

His name was Charles Darwin.

He wrote about how living species change through natural selection.

Your life is not theirs. But a piece of this story may still sit beside you.

You are allowed to need another conversation. You do not have to be certain yet.
