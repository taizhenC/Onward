## Research and scope

Admission came through a cleaning task, while meals, tuition, books, and clothing still required work and other people's support.

## Full reading copy

### 1. scene

He reached a city late at night with a small bag of clothes. He was about sixteen. He was hungry, dirty, and worn out from the road. He had no money left. He had never been in a large city before. He did not know a single person there.

The bag held the few clothes he had brought from home. He had been trying to reach a school where he could study. Part of the distance was still ahead of him. For now, he needed something to eat and somewhere to sleep. He began asking for a room.

### 2. dark_moment

Every place he tried wanted money for a room. He had nothing to pay with. He kept walking through streets he did not know. Food stands had chicken and pies piled up for sale. He wanted that food badly. He could not buy any of it. He could not find another meal elsewhere, either.

Eventually he was too tired to walk farther. A raised stretch of sidewalk left a space beneath the boards. He waited until nobody passing could see him. Then he crawled underneath and lay down on the ground. His bag of clothes became his pillow. Through much of the night, he heard footsteps above his head. He still had nothing to eat.

### 3. response

At daylight, he could see a ship near the place where he had slept. Men were unloading iron from it. He went to the ship and asked its captain for work. He wanted to help unload it so he could buy food. The captain agreed.

He worked until he had earned enough for breakfast. After the hunger of the night, that meal gave him great pleasure. The captain was pleased with his work. He offered to keep him on for a small daily wage. The boy accepted the work.

### 4. struggle

For several days, he worked on the ship. After buying food, very little of his wage remained. He returned to the same space beneath the sidewalk to sleep. Paying for a bed would use money he needed for the road. He kept saving until he thought he had enough. Then he thanked the captain and set out again.

He reached the school with a little money still in his pocket. He went to the head teacher and asked for a class. His clothes and lack of washing made a poor first impression. She admitted other students while he waited nearby. She gave him no answer.

### 5. turning_point

After hours of waiting, the teacher asked him to sweep the next room. The room needed cleaning. He welcomed the task. A household job had taught him how to clean carefully. He swept the floor, then swept it again, and then once more. He dusted the woodwork, benches, tables, and desks. He moved the furniture and cleaned the corners and closets. He went over the surfaces repeatedly with his cloth.

He felt that his future depended on what the teacher found. When he finished, he went to tell her. She inspected the room. She rubbed her handkerchief along the woodwork and over the furniture. She found no dirt or dust. She told him he could enter the school.

### 6. became

He became a student and accepted work cleaning the school. There were many rooms to care for. He worked late, then rose early to build fires and prepare his lessons. The work paid for his meals and room. It could not pay his tuition. The school's leader found someone who would cover that expense.

He borrowed books from students who had them. He still did not have enough clothes. At night, he washed his only pair of socks and dried them by the fire. Some teachers helped him get used clothing. The head teacher gave him advice and encouragement when he found things difficult. He had a place in a classroom. He also had the rooms to clean.

### 7. bridge

His name was Booker Taliaferro Washington.

He helped establish a school where Black students could receive an education and train to become teachers for other schools.
