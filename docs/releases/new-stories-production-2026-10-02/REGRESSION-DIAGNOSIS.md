## Ranked hypotheses

1. **Longer, less discriminating factual context changes rerank comparison and confidence.** New-stage facts range 2,235–4,019 characters, versus original 50 median 1,643. Seven of ten exceed 3,000 characters. The original 104 facts-char p95 rises 10,862 → 14,756 (+35.8%); maximum 11,088 → 16,408. Every failed case gained context even where its two leading candidates retained their order. The source ledger was concatenated into several summaries, including repeated scene details and source/date explanations. `toRerankCandidate` exposes these facts directly while excluding themes, shapes, facets, and story prose. A concise, stage-specific summary may restore useful factual proportions, but this remains a hypothesis until a newly frozen catalog passes a fresh full 104 gate.
2. **Near-tie retrieval and prompt position favor newly adjacent narratives.** This is strongest at index 7: Jacobs now occupies position 1 and describes confinement, escape, distrust, and arrival; Douglass occupies position 2. Index99 also moves Butler 2 → 4 without any keyword signal. Other cases changed lower candidates rather than the gold's score. Facts compression can improve comparison but cannot undo those shortlist positions; freeze the map, themes, ordering, ages, top-six, and gold while measuring the proposed factual summaries.
3. **Existing adjacent-story ambiguity and confidence sensitivity are amplified.** Lee/Graham was already a baseline wrong partial at index 2, while indices 37/38 were correct partials. New 60 turns all three into wrong high-confidence choices. Angelou/Lindgren and Butler/Coleman are already neighboring narratives in both pools. One paired run cannot isolate candidate-context effects from residual provider variation, but the fresh baseline substantially weakens a claim that the new failure is merely an unchanged production failure. Do not rerun unchanged new 60 repeatedly to obtain a favorable receipt.

The hard-confusion metric is still zero because it only counts each frozen case's nominated `plausibleWrong`; Jacobs was not index 7's nominated Butler. This does not erase the wrong definitive match. Preserve all labels and the gate's separate definitive-wrong check.
