### 1. scene

She handed the professor a note of introduction at the college. She was twenty-six. He had shaken her hand warmly and asked what she had studied. She had studied the other subjects, she said, but not surgery. He promised to give her the opportunities for the course. Now he asked her to wait in the room outside the lecture hall.

The students were already assembled inside. She could hear him reading the introduction to them. There was laughter at one point, then applause at the end. She entered quietly and sat among the students. After the lecture, another instructor explained a subject she had missed. Her classes had begun.
