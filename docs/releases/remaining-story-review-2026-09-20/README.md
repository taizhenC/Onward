## What the research changed

Three parallel agents researched and cross-checked the later drafts while the
root worked on the final five, then returned to the old holds. The research
skill required primary-source work and written findings. It led to concrete
corrections, not automatic clearance: conflicting memories stay visible;
reporter narration is not attributed as direct speech; a singing role is not a
dancing role; a figurative speech opening does not establish physical posture;
publication is not evidence of emotional recovery. The reports distinguish
actually read material from search-index leads and failed retrieval attempts.

The archive findings give useful next steps for held stories. They do not prove
the missing events. No restricted item was accessed, purchase made, archive
request sent or unsupported episode silently substituted. The evidence floor
and recipe were not relaxed to make the queue appear finished.
