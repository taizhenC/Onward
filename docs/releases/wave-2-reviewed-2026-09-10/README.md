## Remaining decisions, not word-count padding

- **Berlin:** the [owning 1907 sheet-music record](https://levysheetmusic.mse.jhu.edu/collection/077/181)
  and the attributed first-song account in
  [Radio Stars, August 1934](https://www.worldradiohistory.com/Archive-Radio-Stars/Radio-Stars-1934-08.pdf)
  (printed p.33, final left-column paragraph continuing at top right) support
  collaboration and publication, not private despair. Either select and
  research another episode, exclude it, or explicitly choose a practical-story
  format with suitable matching standards. Do not invent an emotional premise.
- **Brontë:** the real August 26 caregiving letter in
  [Gaskell, volume II, chapter I](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/1700/pg1700-images.html)
  replaces invented bedside writing. The publication story has an ending, but
  the response addresses care rather than writing, and the operation-day
  refusal has no separately attested private reaction. Accept that visible
  tradeoff explicitly or revise from sources; do not invent a feeling.
- **Butler:** the sources support writing during unemployment and different
  outcomes for two manuscripts; they do not establish a single arrival scene,
  order or reaction to the acceptance. The response remains period-level.
  Accept a declared summary-shaped exception or seek a better-sourced episode.
- **Carver:** opening a laundry is documented, but a particular opening session
  is not. The 29-word response is a summary, not a missing paragraph to fill with
  a fictional customer. The remembered home encounter survives through a
  mediated autobiographical sketch in the
  [NPS historic resource study](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/GOVPUB-I29-PURL-gpo236603/pdf/GOVPUB-I29-PURL-gpo236603.pdf)
  (printed pp.72, 78; original-reference note on p.77). Original-manuscript access
  and approximate dating remain limitations. Choose a declared format exception
  or continue bounded episode research.
- **Charles:** the chosen hospital account comes from
  [Aronowitz's attributed interview](https://www.blacklistedjournalist.com/column107.html),
  section II, mother-death answer. It is not combined with the competing
  funeral/crying account, but that choice still needs a reasoned historical
  adjudication; the fear passage must not be certified as independently dated
  to the hospital stay. The 1973 interview mirror and mediated apprenticeship
  material remain uncollated. No new contradiction was discovered in this pass.
- **Christie:** completing the disliked book is resolved before the travel
  turning point. The [estate's book account](https://www.agathachristie.com/news/2024/read-christie-2024)
  (March selection) and [travel account](https://www.agathachristie.com/en/news/2017/christies-travels-on-the-orient-express)
  support the two sequences, not a writing-to-travel recovery cause. Narrow
  to book completion, or explicitly choose a two-part episode. The memoir copy
  also still lacks owning-edition collation. A changed arc needs review of the
  new exact text.
