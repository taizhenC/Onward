existing standard and rewrites for the others. Andersen, Banting and Child

## Source comparisons and corrections

**Berlin:** the attributed first-song answer in
[Radio Stars, August 1934](https://www.worldradiohistory.com/Archive-Radio-Stars/Radio-Stars-1934-08.pdf),
printed p. 33, and the [owning 1907 score](https://levysheetmusic.mse.jhu.edu/collection/077/181)
support collaboration and printed credits, not private despair or the date
when he began using a surname. That unsupported timing was removed. The two
helper episodes in [Woollcott](https://archive.org/download/storyofirvingber00wool/storyofirvingber00wool_djvu.txt),
pp. 67–68, still lack an identified underlying original interview. Later
career episodes were not silently substituted.

**Brontë:** her [1850 Notice](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/771/771-h/771-h.htm),
the consecutive paragraphs beginning “At last” and “As a forlorn hope,” supplies
remembered discouragement tied to the submission. The rewrite says little
hope, not a final attempt. [Shorter, chapter XIII](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/19011/pg19011-images.html),
printed p. 330, supplies the June 16 letter. The relevant July–November 1847
letters and Smith's wrapper anecdote are in
[Gaskell, volume II, chapter II](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/1700/pg1700-images.html);
the Manchester/composition material is in chapter I. The changed locators
correct the earlier chapter-label error, not the frozen historical snapshot.
Contemporary August dispatch evidence controls over the later September
recollection. No original handwriting facsimile was inspected.

**Butler:** the original [Janus 14 interview](https://sf3.org/wp-content/uploads/2012/08/14-Vol-4-No-4.pdf),
Winter 1978/79, printed pp. 30–31, was visually checked in full. The Amber
revision belongs to *Patternmaster*. The prose preserves “about halfway” and
an attempted reorganization, not an exact manuscript position or proven
successful result. The transcript does not date that intervention. The
unemployment/revision placement remains a hold, not an established chronology
created by arranging passages. A conditional editorial-interest letter found
only through a non-owning mirror was not substituted for final acceptance.

**Carver:** the [ECU-held Crisp draft](https://digital.lib.ecu.edu/86436),
0154-s5-b16-fd, typed pp. 49–50 / PDF pp. 59–60, reports the advice request and
visit. It explicitly describes the campus walk together. Its meal sentence
places the visitor with the carpenter's wife in the room where Carver
habitually ate; it does not expressly place him at that particular dinner.
Same-meal claims were removed from the turn and ending.
[Gart, printed p. 84](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/GOVPUB-I29-PURL-gpo236603/pdf/GOVPUB-I29-PURL-gpo236603.pdf)
does explicitly report a shared meal but cites another Crisp draft: this is
an unresolved source trail, not proof that the meal never occurred. The
underlying letter/note 9, conflicting arrival month and room-provider accounts
remain qualified. Archive ownership does not make Crisp an 1891 eyewitness.

**Charles:** [Balliett's attributed interview](https://www.newyorker.com/magazine/1970/03/28/its-detestable-when-you-live-it)
supplies the lodging agreement and later club performance. Listeners are
people from other clubs, not assumed owners. The authorized
[Morrison reprint](https://jazzjournal.co.uk/2020/12/24/jj-12-60-ray-charles-interviewed/)
supplies the food/music material; its inaccurate surrounding narration is
excluded. [Jones's interview](https://achievement.org/achiever/quincy-jones/)
supports the local teaching ending, without adopting conflicting recalled
ages. Omitting disputed crying/hospital sequences does not adjudicate them.

**Christie:** the [memoir copy](https://nibmehub.com/opac-service/pdf/read/agahta%20christie%20_%20an%20autobiography.pdf),
one-based pp. 320–321, supports the garden interruptions, repeated writing,
completion and continuing dislike. The [estate work record](https://www.agathachristie.com/stories/the-mystery-of-the-blue-train)
confirms publication. The writing period is the broader trip, not silently
one hotel stay. Brief memory framing distinguishes recollection from a
contemporary record. The reflowed non-owning memoir copy is still not collated
against an owning edition, and no dispatch gesture or postbox was invented.

## Independent review and release boundary

Three agents authored separate pairs and cross-checked other agents' work:
Brontë/Charles, Carver/Christie, and Berlin/Butler. Their first-pass reports
remain bound to the pre-correction hashes in the local research directory.
The targeted corrections above were then applied, checked and frozen here.
Agent review is not a human approval identity and is never written into the
database's reviewer fields. The research skill materially kept unsupported
claims and incomplete story shapes out of the production subset.

No library, matching description, age-filter configuration, recipe selector,
schema, provider setting, saved reader artifact or 牛大 content is changed.
Several narrowed narratives still differ from existing matching metadata;
alignment requires the separately evaluated figure-library release workflow.
The next set of nine has not been started.

Merging this editorial packet would not publish its JSON files. Eligible
content still needs source/craft clearance and an actual review of the exact
text before the established snapshot-bound promotion procedure. Do not run
blanket seeding or copy simulated validator approval into production.

## Reproducible verification

```sh
node --import tsx scripts/check-story-batch.ts docs/releases/wave-2-rewritten-2026-09-10 6 --require-hashes
npm run check-wave-2-reviewed
npm run lint
npm run typecheck
```

The batch check is read-only: exact file set/hash manifest, strict parsing,
draft and in-memory publication validation, actual canonical composition,
content hashing, serialized replay, and sentence-size/ending constraints.
It loads no credentials, calls no provider or database, and persists no
approvals. CI runs the new packet separately from the unchanged original nine.
These checks test mechanics, not historical truth or editorial readiness.

Local verification passed for all six new candidates (2,708 words) and all
nine unchanged original snapshots. Lint and typecheck passed. Read-only,
in-memory negative tests rejected a changed hash, a missing candidate, a
missing required manifest and an invalid manifest shape, without editing any
snapshot. The direct Node command above also preserves the required-hash flag
on PowerShell hosts whose npm wrapper consumes extra options.
