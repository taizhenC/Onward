## 1. Scene

At her school, she was with two friends and their visiting cousin. She was about twenty-seven. In her memory, they had spent a few hours having fun. The cousin had come for the weekend. She planned to stay at her present school for college. But the visitor thought she belonged at another college. She asked her to come.

## 2. Dark moment

She did not believe she could manage at the college her visitor had suggested. She had heard about its student clubs and their fine clothes. She did not think she could make it there.

## 3. Response

She told her friend's cousin that she did not think she could manage there. She put that doubt into words.

## 4. Struggle

One of her school friends offered a place in her family's home. She also offered to help find work to cover tuition. That summer the young woman moved to the other city. She worked first as a waitress, then doing customers' nails. She gathered enough for the first term and went to register. But her savings had not removed the difficulty. She was still short of money. Her previous school's standing was another problem. There was more preparatory work to do. At registration, she came close to giving up. The first term's money was not enough.

## 5. Turning point

At the new school, she talked with her former night-school teacher. He was there too. She was almost ready to give up. He encouraged her as much as he could. She stayed.

## 6. Became

She completed the missing schoolwork. A year of preparatory study came before she entered college. At her first college assembly, she sat in the chapel and felt taken in. She wanted never to make the school ashamed of her. She had finished the preparatory work, not college. More classes lay ahead.

## 7. Bridge

Her name was Zora Neale Hurston.

She wrote the novel Their Eyes Were Watching God. Your life is not theirs. But a piece of this story may still sit beside you. You are allowed to want something you have wanted for a long time. You do not have to be early.
