## Initial repair decisions

1. **Keep the Morgan encounter honest.** Replacing anticipated fear with actual terror, humiliation, refusal, or a feared exposure of her age would create the very fact the source does not provide. More atmosphere does not solve that evidentiary problem.
2. **Do not move Howard's crisis backward.** It is a useful alternative starting point, but importing it into passage 1 while keeping the existing passage-4 friendship would invert the episode. A new Howard-centered arc would need a genuine intervening struggle and its own complete source-and-craft review. The inspected material did not establish that full replacement chain.
3. **Do not turn compressed trouble into a fabricated occasion.** Removing habitual language from the mathematics account or adding a desk, exam paper, or failed answer would not turn it into a sourced moment.
4. **Do not borrow the hospital scene without a new episode decision.** Its dating and age need verification, and its subject matter would materially change the reader-facing content profile. Even with a date, a medical crisis is not a neutral substitute for the current school/belonging episode.
