## Florence Nightingale

### Actually accessed

[Edward Cook, The Life of Florence Nightingale, volume I (1913)](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/40057/40057-h/40057-h.htm), public-domain HTML, with printed-page anchors and surrounding chapter context. Cook is a later biographer; his embedded dated letters are contemporary primary testimony. Editorial chronology is identified separately.

### Claim-to-source map

- Birth/age: Part I, chapter I, p.4, birth on 12 May 1820.
- Malvern setting, aunt Mai, correspondence with Sutherland: Part III, chapter III, pp.367–368. This is Cook's chronology.
- Scene, low and response share one encounter: Nightingale's reply to Sutherland printed pp.368–370, following his two late-August letters and preceding his 7 September reply. The reply is not independently assigned an exact date.
- The reply reports bodily reactions to walking, driving, food and writing. It also protests advice that does not reach the underlying difficulty. These are her self-reports, not a retrospective diagnosis.
- Repeated failure of the administrative effort: pp.364–366; especially Nightingale to Sir John McNeill, 10 October 1857, describing approved instructions still unissued despite another promise.
- Concrete turn: Nightingale to McNeill, 15 November, p.366. Authorization permits the proposed work and she describes assembling regulations for the committee.
- Bounded became: 1 December letter, p.366, sends rough proofs and asks for severe criticism; Lady Verney to Madame Mohl, 5 December, pp.372–373, reports aunt Mai's account of work and continuing bodily limits. The intermediary chain is retained.
- Bridge occupation/legacy: Part IV, chapter III, pp.440, 445–446. No claim she invented nursing or founded the first-ever training school.

### Craft and safety limits

The scene, low, and small response are not three invented events: they unfold inside one attested reply. No invented visitor, room geometry, private thought, new cause of illness, recovery, or cure. Four disclosed texture sentences elaborate attested states without supplying the dark cause or authorization trigger. The turn is specific permission to undertake an administrative task, not restored health. Continued labor is not prescribed to the reader.
