## Anne Sullivan

### Inspected sources and bounded use

- [Perkins biography](https://www.perkins.org/anne-sullivan/), education paragraphs:
  entry on 7 October 1880 at fourteen, difficult adjustment, later help and progress,
  discipline difficulties, graduation and uncertainty afterward. This is owning-
  institution historical narration, **not** a contemporary diary of her feelings.
- [AFB education exhibit](https://www.afb.org/formative-years/education), learning
  and school paragraphs: institutional narration alongside Sullivan's later
  recollection of the weekly Shakespeare hour, reproduced through Nella Braddy
  Henney's 1933 biography. The recollection is probable, not exact event reporting.
- [Her 1886 valedictory address](https://www.perkins.org/resource/anne-sullivans-valedictory-address/),
  opening, thanks to staff/supporters, classmates and closing: contemporary words
  support an actual graduation address and the substance she expressed.

The draft covers 1880–1886, ages 14–20, consistent with installed bounds. It does
not import the later Keller appointment to manufacture a turn. Nor does a
figurative reference to standing before life's problems establish her physical
posture: that unsupported posture was removed before independent review.

### Decision and next step

**HOLD.** General adjustment and gradual learning do not establish a located
low, her immediate small response or a documented failed effort and return.
A graduation address is real, but the draft must not pretend that graduation
resolved her fear about work and the future. Obtain the school's contemporary
correspondence/disciplinary records and the underlying Sullivan recollections
with page-level context. Alternatively, the owner could choose a different
episode; this packet does not make that choice.

## Maria Tallchief

### Inspected sources and bounded use

- [Oklahoma Historical Society entry](https://www.okhistory.org/publications/enc/entry?entry=TA006),
  birth, 1942 company-entry and name-pressure paragraphs: institutional chronology
  and the refusal to replace her surname. Not a transcript of the exchange.
- [Chicago Film Archives interview log](https://collections.chicagofilmarchives.org/Detail/objects/8944),
  V.2011-05-0602, 28 April 1987: firsthand recollections of company work, a small
  singing role in *Frankie and Johnny*, learning in a Russian-speaking company,
  marriage, Paris work and return in August 1947. The log is not a full video
  transcription. It explicitly distinguishes singing from dancing in that role.
- [Bruce Duffie interview](https://www.bruceduffie.com/tallchief2.html), 1994,
  answer about learning by watching other dancers after finishing a turn:
  a remembered young-dancer habit. It cannot be dated to a particular 1942 class.

The five-year window, ages 17–22, is retained. There is no 1949 *Firebird* scene,
no claim that Ballet Society was founded in 1947, and no assertion that she was
the first American dancer of any kind to perform in Paris.

An important rejected lead: [Debbie Reese's discussion](https://americanindiansinchildrensliterature.blogspot.com/2017/06/some-thoughts-on-chelsea-clintons-she.html)
quotes Tallchief's memoir about school taunting in the childhood section. The
original memoir pages were not collated here. This is a **lead requiring primary
verification**, not evidence to transplant that episode into the 1942 company.

### Decision and next step

**HOLD.** The name decision has a credible historical spine, but its specific
encounter, experienced low, failed effort/return and limited turn remain
insufficiently located. Collate the 1997 autobiography's company-entry pages
against the complete 1987 interview and the OHS interview LL 427. If the
childhood discrimination episode is preferred, it requires an explicit episode
and matching decision, not a silent move across nearly a decade.

## Vera Wang

### Inspected sources and bounded use

- [Vogue India interview, 5 June 2010](https://www.vogue.in/content/meet-vera-wang-bride-maker),
  career and bridal-concept answers: her description of editorial experience,
  accessories design and the kind of bridal clothes she wanted.
- [Harper's Bazaar interview/profile, 24 March 2011](https://www.harpersbazaar.com/fashion/designers/a694/vera-wang-interview/),
  wedding paragraph: the 1989 search, commissioned dress and the reporter's
  claim of paternal funding. Reporter narration is not a recorded shop scene.
- [ELLE interview, 16 March 2023](https://www.elle.com/beauty/makeup-skin-care/a43328070/vera-wang-kohls-aging-interview/),
  marriage/career answer: her approximate recollection of marrying shortly
  before forty. The inspected byline is **Claire Stern Milch**, corrected in the
  candidate citation before review. Her statement that her father would not
  help conflicts with the Bazaar funding account; differing context is possible
  but has not been established. Funding is not asserted as settled in the story.
- [The brand's history](https://www.verawang.com/pages/the-brand), 1990 entry:
  owning-company confirmation of the first bridal flagship.

The draft narrows to 1989–1990, ages 39–41, versus installed 38–41. No precise
wedding birthday is harmonized from conflicting shorthand. No verified source
in this packet establishes the seeded editor-in-chief rejection, so the draft
does not dramatize it or assign it to 1987. The funding conflict is disclosed;
neither solitary self-financing nor paternal funding is treated as settled.

### A promising but unverified lead

A [2006 forum reprint](https://forums.thefashionspot.com/threads/how-i-got-there-vera-wang.90725/)
attributes a first-person lease-signing recollection to **Newsweek**. It mentions
fear and slow early business. It is not an authenticated original interview and
was not used as candidate evidence. The source attribution is no longer unknown:
the next target is *Newsweek*, volume 146, issue 17 (2005), the “How I Got There”
first-person package. A [library-hosted dissertation bibliography](https://www.diva-portal.org/smash/get/diva2%3A639870/ATTACHMENT01.pdf)
indexes that package at pages 48–62; this was a search-result lead, not a read
or verified issue scan. [Contemporaneous journalism criticism](https://www.cjr.org/magazine_report/trendspotting_men_leading_wome.php)
also identifies Wang in the package. Neither secondary item replaces collation
of the published interview.

### Decision and next step

**HOLD.** Dissatisfaction with shopping is not evidence of a deepest low.
The current draft lacks a failed-return sequence, an actual business turning
moment and ordinary early aftermath. Retrieve the Newsweek pages before
considering the lease scene. Even if verified, test the complete arc; one better
scene would not automatically supply every missing beat.

## Bill Wilson

### Inspected sources and bounded use

  further visits and early group. Read as a public transcription of the primary

  Institutional historical chronology, not an eyewitness record of inner life.
- [Dr. Bob's Home, Mayflower Hotel](https://www.drbobshome.org/the-mayflower-hotel/),
  referral-chain account: owning historic-site narration. Its interpretation
  and page-label differences do not supersede the chapter.
- [Glenn C.'s reference guide in the IUSB archive](https://library.iusb.edu/search-find/archives/gcarchive/docs/ref-bbnames.pdf):
  an indexed chronology lead regarding convention dates, not candidate proof
  of a precise final-drink day. No exact final-drink date is asserted.

The text deliberately uses May–August 1935 at age 39 rather than the entire
installed 1934–1935 / ages 38–40 arc. It does not dramatize the disputed hospital
vision chronology or provide medical advice. Shared experience is not framed as
a universal cure or a replacement for medical care.

### Decision and next step

**HOLD.** The return to the hospital is documented, but the chosen turn concerns
another person's willingness; its significance for Wilson's own bounded
difficulty needs a stronger supported connection. The main chapter is a
retrospective, anonymized founding account, and direct owning-page collation
remains pending. Test it against Wilson's own dated descriptions and the
archival chronology. Do not use the later scale of AA as the missing turn.

### Decision and next step

**HOLD.** This is the shortest working draft. The memoir transcript needs
original-edition verification; a walk and eventual publication do not supply the
missing own-response, failed-return and situated-turn sequence. Retrieve the
edited memoir and dated correspondence for this period. A later relationship
or retreat cannot simply be imported to finish the installed stage.
