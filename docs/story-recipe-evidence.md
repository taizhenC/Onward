# Evidence and limits behind the story recipe

Continuation audit, 2026-09-05. The [recipe](../prompts/story-recipe.md) draws
on the earlier five research sweeps and their saved Fable evaluation. The
primary sources below were reopened for this continuation. This is a reading
guide to the evidence, not a claim that the complete Onward format has been
experimentally validated.

| Evidence | What it supports | What remains our decision |
|---|---|---|
| [Mar et al., 2021: narrative versus expository text](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33410100/) ([author-hosted full paper](https://www.yorku.ca/mar/Mar%20et%20al%202021_PB%26R_Mem%20and%20Comp%20of%20N%20and%20E%20texts%20meta-analysis_Print.pdf)) | Across more than 75 samples and 33,000 participants, stories were better remembered and understood than essays. | The study does not compare scenes with summaries within a story. Seven passages, moment-based staging, and word budgets are craft choices. |
| [Ooms, Hoeks & Jansen, 2019: similarity in narrative persuasion](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0215359) | The effects of age similarity differed by reader group; the younger group identified more with a similar-age protagonist. | Age matching is not a universal engagement mechanism. This study does not establish that withholding a historical figure's name improves a comfort story. |
| [Rennick-Egglestone et al., 2019: recovery narratives and recipients](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0226201) | Qualitative accounts identify connection and perceived authenticity as important to the experience of recovery narratives; effects can also be adverse. | These accounts do not validate a fixed plot, a minimum word count, or a promised benefit for an individual reader. We retain real difficulty and avoid turning the figure's outcome into a prediction. |
| [Fazio, Dolan & Marsh, 2015: learning misinformation from fiction](https://www.marshmemorylab.com/s/Fazio-Dolan-Marsh-2015-6g7k.pdf) | Readers reproduced errors from stories despite a pre-reading warning; matched lists produced more suggestibility than stories. Processing mattered more than engagement. | Both conditions received the warning: this does not measure warning effectiveness, post-reading correction, or our disclosure. The paper does not establish a plot-central-error advantage. Our inert-scene boundary is editorial, not a tested intervention. |
| [Peacock, Masullo & Stroud, 2019: story labels and trust](https://mediaengagement.org/research/story-labels-and-trust/) | In the second experiment, correct recall was 66% for an explanatory label and 24% for an above-story label; neither raised trust. | The explanatory label sat between the byline and the article. This was not a test of an after-story drawer. Our visible pointer and itemized disclosure are an application to test, not a location effect established by the study. |

The numbers in the recipe are editorial targets. The reviewed evidence does
not establish 700–950 words, a sixteen-word sentence mean, a twenty-eight-word
ceiling, or a twelve-word final line as optimal for this product. A source gap
must never be filled with invented action to reach those targets. A draft that
cannot satisfy the story shape honestly stays out of publication until its
episode or format is reconsidered.

Likewise, a successful structural validation verifies the document and its
declared evidence links. It does not prove that a source supports a sentence,
that the prose is compelling, or that a person has approved publication. The
first-wave review therefore records source findings and reading findings
separately from the automated result.

## Applied in this continuation

- Add a preface statement that this is a real person's story in our words,
  with the identity at the end. Mention the source record only when one exists.
- Put a short, visible pointer after the completed bridge. Keep the record
  closed until the reader opens it.
- Remove the recipe's contradictory permission example, “You only have to
  begin.” It directs the reader even if a mechanical tone check accepts it.
- Reopen disputed primary sources for the nine candidates. An interrupted
  review is missing evidence, never a passing verdict.

The local candidate files and full source audits remain in
`docs/research/story-specs/`, following the repository's ignored editorial
workspace convention. The reading packet and continuation status there are
for review; draft status and empty publication approvals are preserved.
